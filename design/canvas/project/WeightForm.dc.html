This tool shows where your weight and waist sit against published healthy ranges for adults 18+, using ethnicity-aware BMI cut-offs. It is a screening aid, not a diagnosis, and does not give a diet plan.

About you
Sex
South Asian, Chinese and other Asian backgrounds use lower cut-offs (23 / 27.5) because risk rises at a lower BMI.
Pregnant?
BMI is not used in pregnancy; the tool will stop and refer you to your midwife.
Measurements
Waist-to-height is a better guide than BMI alone for people who carry weight centrally.
Context (optional) Either answer changes how the result is worded. With an eating-disorder history the tool shows no BMI category and points to support instead.
