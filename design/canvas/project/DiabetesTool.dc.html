How this was calculated
The live tool uses QDiabetes-2018 (Model A), validated on 11.5 million people in English general practice, with no blood tests and separate equations for men and women. Because it was built on UK data, the percentage is best calibrated for UK residents; a UK deprivation score is part of the model and a neutral average is assumed.
