The short version

  • Our health tools run in your browser and store none of your answers.
  • If you ask for an email copy of a result, it is sent once and not kept. You give explicit consent for that health information to be emailed.
  • Our analytics count page views and tool completions only. They never receive your answers or results.
  • Members choose what we keep, and can export or delete it at any time.
  • We never use health information for advertising, and we never sell data.
