We can't give you a protein target

In kidney disease, protein guidance runs in the opposite direction to general advice. National kidney guidelines (KDOQI 2020) recommend a reduced, closely supervised protein intake, set individually against your kidney function, dialysis status and whether you have diabetes.

Why there is no number

A general-population figure would not just be unhelpful here. It could cause harm. The right amount depends on things a public calculator cannot see, so this tool refuses to guess. Please ask your renal team or a renal dietitian for a target set for you.

What you can do now

  • Book a review with your renal team or GP if you have not had one recently.
  • Do not increase protein intake on the basis of general fitness advice.

How this was calculated
Every gram-per-kilogram figure traces to a named guideline: IOM/DRI 2005, WHO/FAO/UNU 2007, PROT-AGE 2013, ESPEN 2014, ISSN 2017, ACSM/AND/DC 2016. Bands are collected, the highest applies, a plant-diet multiplier is applied where relevant, the result is capped at 2.0 g/kg and multiplied by your basis weight.
