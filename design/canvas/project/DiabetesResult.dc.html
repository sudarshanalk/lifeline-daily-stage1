How this was calculated
This tool uses QDiabetes-2018 (Model A), a risk equation developed and validated on 11.5 million people in English general practice. It uses no blood tests and has separate equations for men and women, with coefficients for each ethnicity you can select. Because it was built on UK data, the percentage is best calibrated for UK residents. Elsewhere the ranking stays valid but the exact figure may be less precise. A UK deprivation score is part of the model; a neutral average is assumed. Symptoms are checked separately and always take priority over the score. The tool screens for type 2 diabetes only, not type 1 or gestational diabetes, and is not a diagnosis.
