About us

Start small. Change big. Live healthier.

Lifeline Daily turns the evidence on nutrition, movement, sleep and mental well-being into small, doable habits, with a particular focus on South Asian communities in the UK, Sri Lanka and India. Everything we publish is reviewed, dated and sourced.

What we stand for

Evidence first

Every claim traces to a named guideline or peer-reviewed source, listed at the end of the article. If the evidence is thin, we say so.

Reviewed and dated

Articles and tools are checked by a named clinical reviewer, show the review date, and are re-reviewed on a schedule or when guidance changes.

Your data stays yours

Our tools store nothing. Members choose what we keep and can export or delete it at any time. Health data is never used for advertising.

Editorial and clinical review policy

Authors draft. An editor checks structure, sources and plain language. A clinical reviewer checks accuracy, safety messaging and whether the piece could mislead someone with a condition. Only then is it published, with the reviewer's name and date on the page.

Health tools go further: the method, its sources, its limits and the exact deployed version are recorded in an approval record before release. Any change to a calculation needs a new approval. If a concern is raised, the tool is paused while we check.

Corrections are logged on the article. We do not accept sponsored health claims and we do not run advertising targeted on health information.
