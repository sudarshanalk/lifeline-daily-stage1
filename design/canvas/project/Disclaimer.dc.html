What Lifeline Daily is

Lifeline Daily publishes educational articles and offers self-service health tools. Our content is written by qualified authors and medically reviewed, and each page shows its review date. It is general information for adults and is not tailored to you.

What it is not

  • Not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
  • Not a substitute for a consultation with a doctor, nurse, dietitian or pharmacist who knows your history.
  • Not an emergency service. Nobody monitors tool results or messages in real time.

About our health tools

Each tool is a screening aid. It estimates a figure from published guidelines or validated models using only what you enter. It cannot see your blood results, medication or medical history, and it cannot detect a condition you already have.

Daily Protein Target gives a general-health target for adults 18+. It gives no figure to people with kidney disease, whose intake must be set by their renal team. Diabetes Risk Check estimates 10-year risk of type 2 diabetes with QDiabetes-2018 (Model A). It does not assess type 1 or gestational diabetes. A blood test interpreted by a clinician is required to diagnose diabetes. Symptoms always take priority over the score. Tools are paused whenever our clinical reviewers are checking them, and the page says so.

When to see a clinician

  • Any tool shows a high or very high result, or tells you to seek testing.
  • You have symptoms such as excessive thirst, passing urine very often, unexplained weight loss, blurred vision or slow-healing cuts.
  • You are pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, or living with a diagnosed condition, and want to change what you eat or how you exercise.

Questions or concerns

If you believe something on this site is wrong or unsafe, please contact us. Clinical concerns are reviewed by our named clinical reviewer and may lead to a tool being paused while we check.
