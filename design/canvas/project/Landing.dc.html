Daily Protein Target

Available now

A guideline-based estimate of how much protein you need each day, and roughly how to spread it across meals. Takes about two minutes. Nothing you enter is stored.

Reviewed by [Clinical reviewer] on [date]·Method version a10d21b0

Purpose

This tool estimates a daily protein target for general health. It collects every published intake band that applies to you, takes the most demanding one, adjusts for a plant-based diet, caps the result at 2.0 g per kg, and multiplies by a sensible basis weight.

Who it is for

  • Adults aged 18 and over.
  • Not for children, who have separate age-banded requirements.
  • Not for people with kidney disease. National kidney guidelines recommend a reduced, supervised intake, so the tool gives no number and refers you to your renal team.

What you will enter

AgeSexHeight & weight (any units)EthnicityActivity levelResistance trainingGoalDietMeals per dayKidney & liver statusPregnancy / breastfeeding

Limitations

  • A target for general health, not a prescription. The tool cannot see blood results, medication or medical history.
  • The plant-diet adjustment is an extrapolation from digestibility data, not a guideline figure, and is labelled as such in the result.
  • The 2.0 g/kg ceiling is deliberate. Higher figures exist only for supervised athletes in a calorie deficit.

How it works

Every gram-per-kilogram figure traces to a named source. Nothing is invented or interpolated beyond what the source states.
