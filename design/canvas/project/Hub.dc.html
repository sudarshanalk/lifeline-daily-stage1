Health Tools

Free, evidence-based checks you can do in a few minutes. No account, nothing stored. Every tool names its method and its limits, and none of them is a diagnosis.

Daily Protein Target

Estimates a daily protein target from published intake guidelines (IOM, WHO, ISSN, ESPEN, PROT-AGE) and splits it across your meals. Adjusts for activity, training, goals, age and diet.

Takes
About 2 minutes
Who it is for
Adults 18 and over
You will enter
Age, sex, height, weight, activity, training, goal, diet, meals, kidney and liver status

Not for people with kidney disease, whose protein needs must be set by their renal team. The tool says so and gives no number in that case.

Diabetes Risk Check

Your estimated 10-year risk of developing type 2 diabetes, using QDiabetes-2018 (Model A), a model built on 11.5 million UK patient records that needs no blood tests. Includes a symptom check and two cross-checks.

Takes
About 4 minutes
Who it is for
Adults 25 to 84
You will enter
Age group, sex, height, weight, ethnicity, smoking, family and medical history, optional waist, symptoms

A screening aid, not a diagnosis. It does not assess type 1 or gestational diabetes. Symptoms always take priority over the score.

How our tools work

Named methods

Every number traces to a published guideline or validated model. Each tool's page names its method, version and limits.

Nothing stored

Calculations run in your browser. We keep no record of your answers. If you ask for an email copy, it is sent once and not kept.

Not a diagnosis

These are screening aids. A clinician and, where relevant, a blood test are needed to diagnose anything. When to seek urgent care
