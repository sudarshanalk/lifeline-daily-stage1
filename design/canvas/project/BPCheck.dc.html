Home reading in the high range

For home measurements, NICE treats an average of 135/85 or more as high blood pressure. A single reading is not enough to say that; it is the average over several days that matters.

under 135/85135–149 / 85–94150–179 / 95–119180/120+

Next step

Log readings morning and evening for 4–7 days. If your average stays at 135/85 or above, book a GP or pharmacy blood pressure check and take the log with you.

Seek same-day care if a reading is 180/120 or higher, or if you have chest pain, shortness of breath, a severe headache, vision changes or weakness on one side. Urgent care numbers
How this was categorised
Categories follow NICE NG136 (2019, updated 2023) home blood pressure thresholds: stage 1 from 135/85, stage 2 from 150/95, severe from 180/120 (clinic). The tool categorises single readings for information and your 7-day average for the next-step advice.
