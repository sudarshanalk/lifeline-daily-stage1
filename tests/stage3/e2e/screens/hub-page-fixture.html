  • 🩺⏱ 3 min check

    Diabetes Risk Check

    Estimate your risk of developing type 2 diabetes over the next 10 years and see which of your own factors drive it.

    Available now

    About 3 minutes

    • Age band and sex
    • Height, weight and waist
    • Ethnicity and smoking
    • Family history and medical history
    • Current symptoms

    Adults aged 25 to 84 without a diagnosis of diabetes. Not a diagnosis; symptoms are handled separately from the score.

    Shows the factors behind your risk

    Check my risk
  • ❤️⏱ 3 min check

    Heart attack and stroke risk check

    Estimates your chance of a heart attack or stroke in the next 10 years, and which change would lower it most.

    Available now

    About 3 minutes

    • Age, sex and country
    • Smoking and diabetes
    • Blood pressure (top number)
    • Cholesterol, or height and weight

    Adults aged 40 to 74 who have not had a heart attack, stroke or heart surgery. Not a diagnosis.

    Shows which change would lower your risk most

    Check my heart risk
  • 🩸⏱ 1 min check

    Blood pressure reading check

    Shows what your blood pressure reading means, and when to get it checked.

    Available now

    About 1 minute

    • Your reading (top and bottom numbers)
    • Pulse (optional)
    • Pregnancy and blood pressure medicine

    Adults aged 18 and over. Not a diagnosis.

    Says when to get your reading checked

    Check my reading
  • 🧪⏱ 3 min check

    Metabolic health check

    Checks your waist, blood pressure and blood test results against the International Diabetes Federation definition of metabolic syndrome.

    Available now

    About 3 minutes

    • Age, sex and family background
    • Waist
    • Blood pressure
    • Blood test results, if you have them

    Adults aged 18 and over. Not a diagnosis.

    Explains each result against its threshold

    Check my results
  • 🏃⏱ 2 min check

    Fitness age

    Estimates your fitness level (VO₂max) from a few questions, the age it is typical of, and what would raise it.

    Available now

    About 2 minutes

    • Age and sex
    • Waist
    • Resting heart rate
    • How often, how hard and how long you exercise

    Adults aged 18 and over. Not a diagnosis.

    Shows what would raise your fitness

    Find my fitness age
  • 💓⏱ 1 min check

    Heart-rate training zones

    Works out your easy, moderate and hard heart-rate zones for exercise, with a simple talk test for each.

    Available now

    About 1 minute

    • Age
    • Resting heart rate (optional)
    • Measured maximum heart rate (optional)

    Adults aged 18 and over.

    Includes a talk test for each zone

    Sleep and stress check

    A few short questions about stress and worry, mood and sleep. The result shows whether any of them is worth attention and where to find help.

    Available now

    About 2 minutes

    • Stress and worry
    • Mood
    • Sleep

    Adults. Not a diagnosis.
